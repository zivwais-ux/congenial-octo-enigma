## 1. Summary

We built Street Eats Asia to help travelers share hyperlocal recommendations.
We collect the minimum data needed to run that service and never sell your
personal data.

## 2. Information we collect

- **Account data:** display name, email, and (if you use them) Google/Apple
  sign-in identifiers. Optional profile fields: photo, bio, home base.
- **Content you create:** recommendations (title, description, category, rating,
  cost, tags), photos you upload, and the location you attach to a post.
- **Location data:** only when you tap "Use my current location", and only to
  set a pin for a post or to sort nearby results. Location is never collected in
  the background.
- **Usage & device data:** app interactions and basic device/diagnostic
  information for analytics and crash reporting (see §5).

## 3. How we use it

- To operate the App: publish your recommendations, power the feed, search, map,
  and profiles.
- To keep the community safe: review flagged content and enforce our Terms.
- To improve the App: aggregate, de-identified analytics.
- To communicate essential service messages.

We do **not** use precise location for advertising, and we do not sell personal
data.

## 4. Legal bases (where applicable, e.g. GDPR)

Performance of a contract (providing the App), our legitimate interests
(security, product improvement), your consent (camera, photos, precise
location), and legal obligations.

## 6. Sharing & visibility

Recommendations, photos, ratings, your display name and profile are **public**
within the App by design. Do not post anything you want to keep private. Your
email and precise location are not shown to other users.

## 7. Data retention

We keep your account and content until you delete them. You can delete your own
recommendations in-app; to delete your account and associated data, contact
[privacy@yourdomain.com].
